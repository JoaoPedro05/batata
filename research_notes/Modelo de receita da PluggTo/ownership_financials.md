# Plugg.To: ownership chain (Linx, StoneCo, TOTVS) and how its revenue is reported

Research date: 2026-10-07. Dates are flagged throughout.

**Correction to the brief's premise:** The brief describes the sequence as "Linx acquired Plugg.To, then StoneCo acquired Linx." The order was the other way around. StoneCo agreed to buy Linx in 2020 and closed in 2021, with Linx consolidated from 3Q21. Linx bought Plugg.To in **June 2022**, while it was already a StoneCo subsidiary. In **July 2025** StoneCo agreed to sell most of Linx's software to **TOTVS**, and that sale **closed on 2026-02-27**.

Ownership chain:
- Plugg.To was independent from 2013 to 2022, with Wayra and the Mercado Livre fund as investors from 2015.
- Linx owned it from June 2022, with StoneCo as Linx's parent.
- TOTVS owns it from 2026-02-27, very likely: TOTVS bought "e-commerce (front e back) e Omnichannel", but Plugg.To is not named; see Q4.

## Q1. When, and for how much, did Linx acquire Plugg.To? What revenue and clients were disclosed at the time?

### Takeaway
Linx (owned by StoneCo) announced the acquisition of Plugg.To on **14 June 2022**. It was an all-cash deal, and the price and terms were **not disclosed**. At the time Plugg.To had about **R$10.8 million in annual revenue**, about **1,900 clients**, **70 integrated marketplaces** and **2.9 billion requests per month**.

### Cited Findings
- Linx announced the acquisition of Plugg.to, which it described as a hub for integrations between online store platforms, ERPs, suppliers and marketplaces. The price and terms were not disclosed. — [Baguete, 14 Jun 2022](https://www.baguete.com.br/noticias/linx-compra-plugg-to)
- Plugg.To's figures at the time of the deal — [Baguete, 14 Jun 2022](https://www.baguete.com.br/noticias/linx-compra-plugg-to):
  - R$10.8 million in annual revenue
  - about 1,900 clients
  - 70 integrated marketplaces
  - 2.9 billion requests per month
  - founded in 2013, with investment from Wayra and the Mercado Livre investment fund in early 2015
  - CEO and founder: André Fuhrman
- Context in the same article — [Baguete](https://www.baguete.com.br/noticias/linx-compra-plugg-to):
  - This was Linx's first acquisition of a controlling stake since Stone bought it (Stone paid R$6.8 billion in 2020).
  - Linx's previous deal was a 40% stake in Neomode for R$7 million in July 2021.
  - Linx said it would use its network of 700 franchises and 1,300 sales executives to sell Plugg.To.
- A second source says the deal was paid in cash and that the amount was not made public. — [Guia do Investidor](https://guiadoinvestidor.com.br/?p=133883) (search snippet only)
- Other coverage of the same deal: [Mercado & Consumo, 14 Jun 2022](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/14/06/2022/noticias/linx-especializada-em-tecnologia-para-o-varejo-compra-hub-de-marketplace-plugg-to/); [IT Forum](https://itforum.com.br/linx-adquire-hub-de-marketplaces-plugg-to/); [GiroNews](https://gironews.com/varejo-digital/linx-compra-pluggto-68649/)

### Inferences
- The deal was too small to require separate disclosure. Plugg.To's ~R$10.8M was under 1% of Linx's roughly R$1.1bn in annual revenue, which is why there is no CVM or SEC material-fact filing for it.
- A typical SaaS revenue multiple at the time would imply a price in the tens of millions of reais. This is speculation, not a disclosed figure.

### Gaps
- No price, earn-out or deal structure was found in any StoneCo 6-K or 20-F, or in the press.
- The year the R$10.8M revenue refers to (2021 or the trailing twelve months) was not specified.

## Q2. How is Plugg.To / marketplace hub revenue classified in Linx and StoneCo reporting: SaaS subscription or transactional?

### Takeaway
StoneCo's management segmentation:
- From 4Q21, StoneCo split its **Software segment** into **"Core"** and **"Digital"**.
- **"Marketplace Hub"** is explicitly listed under **Digital**, alongside OMS, the Linx Commerce e-commerce platform, engagement tools (Linx Impulse, mLabs) and Ads.
- The Marketplace Hub line already existed before Plugg.To was acquired, so Plugg.To was presumably folded into it.

StoneCo's IFRS income statement:
- Software revenue is mainly recorded under **"Net revenue from subscription services and equipment rental"**.
- This is a fixed monthly fee recognized over time, plus non-recurring services such as implementation and training.
- Neither Linx nor StoneCo ever reported Plugg.To's revenue separately.

### Cited Findings
- **Segment definition (4Q21 release, March 2022):** StoneCo grouped Linx and its other software investments into one Software Division with three fronts — [StoneCo 4Q21/FY21 release, SEC 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1745431/000095010322004753/dp169324_ex9901.htm):
  1. **Core:** POS/ERP, TEF and QR Code gateways, reconciliation and CRM
  2. **Digital:** "(i) our omnichannel platform (OMS); (ii) our e-commerce platform (Linx Commerce), (iii) engagement tools (Linx Impulse and mlabs); (iv) our Ads solution and (v) Marketplace Hub"
  3. **Financial services:** Linx sub-acquiring, which was being discontinued
- **Same definition in later releases:** The 1Q22 and 2Q22 releases repeat it: "(ii) Digital, which includes OMS, e-commerce platform, engagement tools, Ads solution and Marketplace Hub." The 2Q22 release covers the quarter in which Plugg.To was bought. — [StoneCo 1Q22 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1745431/000095010322010046/dp174615_ex9901.htm); [StoneCo 2Q22 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1745431/000095010322014192/dp178849_ex9901.htm)
- **Recurring share of Core:** "In Linx, 85.7% of core revenues are recurring revenues, which are monthly subscription fees related to software usage and ongoing technical support." The retention rate in the POS/ERP business was 98.9%. — [StoneCo 4Q21 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1745431/000095010322004753/dp169324_ex9901.htm)
- **Digital growth:** "Linx Digital business grew revenue by 20.0% year over year, with double digit growth in e-commerce solutions, OMS and Ads." — [StoneCo 4Q21 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1745431/000095010322004753/dp169324_ex9901.htm)
  - Note: a web-search summary labelled this as "Q3 2022", but the filing is the 4Q21/FY2021 release.
  - The 1Q22 release cited the "low maturity of our Digital business, with higher cloud costs" as a drag on margins. — [StoneCo 1Q22 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1745431/000095010322010046/dp174615_ex9901.htm)
- **Software revenue scale** — [1Q22 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1745431/000095010322010046/dp174615_ex9901.htm); [2Q22 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1745431/000095010322014192/dp178849_ex9901.htm):
  - 1Q22: R$326.6 million, of which Linx contributed R$256.3 million
  - 2Q22: R$350.7 million, up 23.3% pro-forma for Linx
- **When Linx entered StoneCo's numbers:** StoneCo consolidated Linx "beginning in 3Q21." — [1Q22 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1745431/000095010322010046/dp174615_ex9901.htm)
- **IFRS revenue lines:** StoneCo's statement of profit or loss reports:
  - "Net revenue from transaction activities and other services"
  - "Net revenue from subscription services and equipment rental"
  - financial income
  - other financial income

  The subscription line jumped after Linx was consolidated: R$408.1M in 4Q21 against R$122.0M in 4Q20. — [StoneCo 4Q21 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1745431/000095010322004753/dp169324_ex9901.htm)
- **Revenue recognition policy (FY2025 financial statements, note 17.1.1.2):** For software solutions, the performance obligations are — [StoneCo FY2025 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1745431/000207097926000025/stoneco_12x2025.htm):
  - "(a) recurring subscription services, such as reconciliation, business automatization solutions, services to provide the client with the right of use of software in a cloud-based [model]..., technological support, help desk... and software hosting services"
  - "(b) non-recurring services, such as implementation services, personalization, training"
  - "Subscription service fees are charged as a fixed monthly fee, and the related revenue is recognized over time"
- **Linx revenue under TOTVS's deal perimeter:** 2024 total revenue was R$1,145M, of which **R$1,048M (over 90%) was recurring**. Adjusted EBITDA was R$239M (20.9% margin). — [TOTVS Fato Relevante, 22 Jul 2025](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/d3be5d49-62e7-4def-a3e1-ab25ff09f153/f12a478d-64e3-6976-3e6b-df2be49b3482?origin=1)
- **Bridge from StoneCo's segment to the deal perimeter (2024):** StoneCo's Software segment revenue was R$1,563M. The R$1,145M perimeter is what remains after deducting about R$418M for assets outside the perimeter and other net adjustments. — [TOTVS Fato Relevante annex, p.8](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/d3be5d49-62e7-4def-a3e1-ab25ff09f153/f12a478d-64e3-6976-3e6b-df2be49b3482?origin=1)

### Inferences
- In StoneCo's management reporting, Plugg.To's revenue would have sat in **Software → Digital → Marketplace Hub**.
- Under IFRS, its monthly platform fees almost certainly fall in **"subscription services"**, recognized over time. Any setup or onboarding fees would be non-recurring services.
- It is unclear whether any GMV- or order-based fees were booked as subscription or as "transaction activities and other services". No filing addresses this specifically.
- StoneCo appears to have stopped reporting the Core/Digital split in later releases, at least by 2023–2024, when Software was presented as a single segment. This was not verified line by line.

### Gaps
- No Plugg.To or Marketplace Hub revenue figure was found in any StoneCo filing. Searching SEC full text for "Plugg" in StoneCo filings returned no hits.
- Digital revenue was never disclosed as an absolute figure in the releases reviewed; only growth rates were given.
- Linx's own reporting before the acquisition (ri.linx.com.br, 2019–2020) predates Plugg.To and was not reviewed.

## Q3. Are there disclosed Plugg.To operating metrics (GMV, sellers, channels)?

### Takeaway
The only hard metrics found date from June 2022: about 1,900 clients, 70 marketplaces, 2.9 billion API requests per month and R$10.8M in annual revenue. No Black Friday GMV figure (for example "R$500 milhões") was found in any reliable source, so treat any such number as unverified.

### Cited Findings
- June 2022: 1,900 clients, 70 integrated marketplaces, 2.9 billion requests per month, R$10.8M annual revenue. — [Baguete](https://www.baguete.com.br/noticias/linx-compra-plugg-to)
- **Black Friday 2022:** E-Commerce Brasil covered Plugg.To's new panel and AI tools for Black Friday 2022, calling it one of the largest marketplace integration platforms in Brazil. It gave no GMV or order figures. — [E-Commerce Brasil](https://www.ecommercebrasil.com.br/noticias/plugg-to-novas-ferramentas-black-friday)
- **Amazon recognition (January 2024):** Plugg.To received the "Empresa Conectada ao Cliente" badge in Amazon.com.br's partner awards. — [PR Newswire Brasil](https://prnewswire.com/br/comunicados-para-a-imprensa/pluggto-recebe-distintivo-de-empresa-conectada-ao-cliente-em-premiacao-da-amazoncombr-302043812.html)
- **Microvix integration (undated):** Linx launched a Microvix ERP feature built on Plugg.To technology. It lets small and mid-sized retailers connect to "more than 70 marketplaces." — [Central do Varejo](https://centraldovarejo.com.br/linx-lanca-nova-funcionalidade-para-gestao-de-marketplaces/?amp=1)
- **TikTok Shop integration (undated, probably 2025):** Retailers using Plugg.To, described as "a Linx solution specialized in marketplaces," could connect their catalogs to TikTok Shop. The source quotes the executive Daniel Mendez. — this came from a search-result summary; the original URL was not confirmed.

### Inferences
- Plugg.To kept operating as a branded Linx product after 2022. It was also built into Linx's ERP (Microvix) as its marketplace connector, which supports the view that it was absorbed into Linx's Digital/e-commerce portfolio.

### Gaps
- No GMV processed, seller count or channel count after 2022 was found.
- No Black Friday GMV figure was found for any year (2022–2025). If the "R$500 milhões na Black Friday" figure exists, it likely comes from a Plugg.To or Linx press release not indexed in these searches.

## Q4. What happened to Linx/StoneCo software, and is Plugg.To part of it as of 2026?

### Takeaway
StoneCo put Linx up for sale in 2024 and signed a deal on **21–22 July 2025** to sell Linx and other software assets to **TOTVS**:
- Enterprise value: **R$3.05bn**
- Total including roughly R$360M of net cash: about R$3.41bn
- The sale **closed on 27 Feb 2026**, and StoneCo says it received **R$3.272bn**

TOTVS's deal perimeter includes "**e-commerce (front e back) e Omnichannel**" and the legal entity **Linx Commerce Ltda.**, which is where Plugg.To most plausibly sits. The excluded list does not mention Plugg.To. So Plugg.To is **very likely now owned by TOTVS** and reported inside the "Gestão" business unit. However, **no source names Plugg.To explicitly** in the perimeter.

### Cited Findings
**2024 sale process**
- StoneCo hired Morgan Stanley and J.P. Morgan to sell Linx. TOTVS and Constellation Software were reported among the interested bidders. — [Central do Varejo](https://centraldovarejo.com.br/linx-e-colocada-a-venda-pela-stone/?amp=1)
- In November 2024, Reuters reported that initial non-binding offers came in below what Stone paid in 2020. — [Money Times/Reuters](https://www.moneytimes.com.br/primeiras-ofertas-pela-linx-nao-impressionam-stone-dizem-fontes-mtm); [TradingView/Reuters](https://de.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2024:newsml_L2N3MY0WK:0-stone-unimpressed-by-non-binding-offers-for-linx-sources-say)

**Original 2020–2021 purchase by Stone**
- Stone's purchase of Linx was approved by Linx shareholders for about R$6.7bn (2020). — [InfoMoney](https://www.infomoney.com.br/?p=1547636)
- CADE's General Superintendence cleared it without restrictions on 19 Mar 2021. — [Mercado & Consumo](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/2021/03/20/superintendencia-do-cade-aprova-aquisicao-da-linx-pela-stone/)
- StoneCo consolidated Linx from 3Q21. — [StoneCo 1Q22 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1745431/000095010322010046/dp174615_ex9901.htm)

**TOTVS Fato Relevante (22 Jul 2025)** — [TOTVS Fato Relevante + annex](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/d3be5d49-62e7-4def-a3e1-ab25ff09f153/f12a478d-64e3-6976-3e6b-df2be49b3482?origin=1)
- TOTVS agreed to buy 100% of Linx Participações S.A. from Linx S.A., a StoneCo subsidiary. At closing, Linx Participações would hold "parte do segmento de negócio de software da Linx" (part of Linx's software business).
- Price: R$3,050,000,000, adjusted for cash and net debt, with no earn-out.
- Implied multiples: 2.7x EV/Sales and 12.7x EV/EBITDA.
- **Inside the perimeter:** "Farmácias, Redes de Fast Food, Concessionária de Veículos, Moda, Lojas de Material de Construção, Franquias, Postos de Combustíveis, Saúde, RH, Educação, Napse, **e-commerce (front e back) e Omnichannel**"
- **Outside the perimeter:** "SimplesVet, Reclame Aqui, Dental, Linx Pay, **Linx Impulse**, Alpha-Logo, APP Sistemas, Hiper Software, MLabs Software, Trinks, VHSYS e Questor"
- Plugg.To and "marketplace hub" appear in neither list.

**StoneCo's FY2025 financial statements (6-K, 2026)** — [StoneCo FY2025 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1745431/000207097926000025/stoneco_12x2025.htm)
- In 2Q25, StoneCo classified "Linx Sistemas e Consultoria Ltda and certain other software assets" (the "Software Businesses") as held for sale and as discontinued operations.
- The listed entities are:
  - Linx Software Participações em Tecnologia S.A.
  - Linx Sistemas e Consultoria
  - Linx Telecomunicações
  - Linx Automotivo
  - **Linx Commerce Ltda.**
  - Linx People
  - Linx Saúde
  - Sponte Educação
  - Napse (Argentina, Uruguay, Chile)
  - Synthesis Holding/US
  - Retail Americas (Mexico)
- The sale "was approved without restrictions by... CADE on January 30, 2026, and completed on February 27, 2026. The total amount received was R$ 3,272,193" thousand.
- Entities StoneCo keeps:
  - **Stone Impulse Ltda. ("Formerly Linx Impulse Ltda.")**
  - **Stone Corporate ("Formerly Linx S.A.")**
  - Trinks
  - VHSYS (50%)
  - Reclame Aqui (50%)
- SimplesVet was sold separately in July 2025, with a gain of R$56.6M.
- **Date conflict:** The 6-K gives CADE clearance as 30 Jan 2026, while a press summary of StoneCo's closing release says 20 Feb 2026. The closing date of 27 Feb 2026 is consistent across sources. — [Observer-Reporter/Newsfile, 27 Feb 2026](https://stocks.observer-reporter.com/observerreporter/article/newsfile-2026-2-27-stoneco-announces-successful-closing-of-linx-sale)
- StoneCo's 2025 release put the total at R$3.05bn EV plus about R$360M of estimated net cash, roughly R$3.41bn. About R$3.8bn of fiscal goodwill from the original Linx purchase stays with StoneCo. — StoneCo FY2025 6-K / July 2025 announcement as summarized in search results ([6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1745431/000162828025038868/stoneco_06x2025.htm))
  - Press coverage variously cites R$3.05bn (EV) and R$3.42bn (including cash). — [IstoÉ Dinheiro](https://istoedinheiro.com.br/totvs-anuncia-compra-da-linx-em-negocio-de-r-305-bilhoes); [B3 Bora Investir, 22 Jul 2025](https://borainvestir.b3.com.br/noticias/empresas/totvs-compra-a-linx-da-stone-em-negocio-de-r-305-bilhoes/)
- Press framed the sale as a loss of more than half the value Stone paid ("deságio de ~54%"). — [Mercado & Consumo, 23 Jul 2025](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/23/07/2025/noticias/stone-vende-empresa-de-software-por-menos-da-metade-do-que-pagou/amp/)

**TOTVS 1Q26 ITR** — [TOTVS 1Q26 ITR](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/d3be5d49-62e7-4def-a3e1-ab25ff09f153/d9a8ce5b-9d48-bec8-a967-82ebe69405d7?origin=2)
- The acquisition of Linx Software Participações em Tecnologia S.A. closed on 27 Feb 2026, so 1Q26 includes only March 2026 of Linx.
- Linx was placed in the **TOTVS Gestão** business unit.
- Linx's March 2026 figures: about R$100M of net revenue, of which R$93M was recurring, and R$18M of adjusted EBITDA.
- Linx and TBDC together contributed R$106.9M of revenue in the quarter.
- The subsidiaries list includes Linx Commerce Ltda. No Plugg.To entity is listed.

### Inferences
- **Plugg.To most likely transferred to TOTVS** on 27 Feb 2026, for three reasons:
  1. StoneCo's own taxonomy placed Marketplace Hub in "Digital" alongside e-commerce and OMS, and the TOTVS perimeter includes "e-commerce (front e back) e Omnichannel."
  2. Linx Commerce Ltda. is among the entities sold.
  3. Plugg.To is not on the excluded list, and it had been built into Microvix, a POS/ERP that was transferred.
- The one Digital asset StoneCo kept is Linx Impulse (now Stone Impulse, covering engagement and Ads), along with mLabs.
- It is not confirmed whether Plugg.To's legal entity, if a separate one survived, was merged into Linx Commerce or Linx Sistemas before the sale.
- Under TOTVS, Plugg.To revenue would now be reported within the **Gestão** unit's recurring revenue and is not separately disclosed.

### Gaps
- No primary document names Plugg.To as inside or outside the TOTVS perimeter. Checking the CADE filing (Ato de Concentração for TOTVS/Linx) or the Plugg.To / TOTVS websites for current branding would confirm this.
- It is not clear whether Plugg.To's legal entity (its CNPJ) was merged into a Linx entity after June 2022.
- No 2026 information was found on TOTVS's plans for Plugg.To, such as a rebrand or integration with TOTVS's own marketplace connectors.
