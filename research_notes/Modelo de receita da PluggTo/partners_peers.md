# Plugg.To: Partner/Secondary Revenue Sources and Peer Pricing Models (Brazil, as of Oct 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-07. Several Plugg.To pages (plugg.to/planos, plugg.to/pluggto-partners) returned HTTP 503 during research, so some Plugg.To details come from search snippets, not from reading the full pages.

## Q1. Does Plugg.To earn revenue from carriers, marketplaces or partners, or are partnerships only for customer acquisition?

### Takeaway
I found no public evidence that Plugg.To earns revenue share from carriers or marketplaces. The partner programs it publishes are outbound referral programs: Plugg.To pays agencies, consultants and resellers rebates or progressive commissions for bringing in sellers. So the partnerships look like customer-acquisition channels (a cost), not secondary revenue. Logistics partners (Total Express, Frenet, Frete Rápido, Intelipost and others) show up as integrations, and I found no disclosed commercial terms for any of them.

### Cited Findings
- **Agency program (outbound commissions).** Plugg.To's "Programa Agências" is for "agências, revendedores e influencers do ecossistema digital Linx Commerce". It is part of Linx's "Commerce Partner Program" and pays "comissões progressivas conforme o volume de indicações", along with training and support. — [Plugg.To – Programa Agências](https://plugg.to/programa-agencias/)
- **Partners program.** Benefits listed are "Receba um rebate por indicações realizadas por você", "Desconto exclusivo para os seus clientes" and a specialized team. The FAQ says a partner "pode ser bonificado por novos clientes conectados a Plugg.To" if it follows the referral rules. The money flows from Plugg.To to the partner. The page metadata suggests it is about 673 days old (circa late 2024), and the full page returned 503 when fetched. — [Plugg.To Partners](https://plugg.to/pluggto-partners/); see also [Programa Parcerias](https://plugg.to/programa-parcerias/)
- **Logistics partners.** Plugg.To lists Total Express with other logistics partners: "Intelipost, frenet, total express, frete rápido, meus correios, frete barato, central do frete, visualset, flixlog, SGP Web". — [Plugg.To – Marketplace de integração](https://plugg.to/marketplace-de-integracao/) (via search snippet)
- **Frete Rápido.** The integration lets a merchant get freight quotes on every marketplace it uses through Plugg.To, drawing on Frete Rápido's partner carriers. The page describes it as a technical integration and gives no commercial terms. The page is older and undated. — [Frete Rápido](https://freterapido.com.br/integracao-frete-rapido-e-plugg-to-logistica-4-0-nos-maiores-marketplaces-do-pais/)
- **Frenet.** Frenet's help center documents a Plugg.To integration that brings Frenet's delivery options to marketplaces. The article was updated in November 2025. — [Frenet help](https://ajuda.frenet.com.br/knowledge-base/2160328-configuracao-plugg-to)
- **Mercado Livre Developer Partner Program.** Certified integrators are ranked in levels (Certificado, Silver, Gold, Platinum) on 5 requirements, including the GMV of active users. Benefits are non-monetary: exposure in the Central de Parceiros, faster integrator support (2 days, 1 day or 3 hours depending on level) and an Integration Expert focal point. I found no cash revenue share for integrators. — [Mercado Livre Developer Partner Program](https://developers.mercadolivre.com.br/pt_br/developer-partner-program)
- **Certification costs.** Under Mercado Libre's developer terms, developers bear the costs of certification. — [Mercado Libre Global Selling Developer T&C](https://global-selling.mercadolibre.com/devsite/mercado-libre-global-selling-developer-terms-and-conditions)
- **Mercado Livre consultancy program.** The "Programa de Parceiro Certificado" for consultancies is a separate program. It has quarterly reviews, requires advising sellers with more than 5 monthly sales, and has more than 300 certified partners. I saw no payment terms. — [ML Termos Parceiro Certificado](https://partners.mercadolivre.com.br/tyc-consultant-portugues/)
- **Mercado Pago contrast.** The ecosystem's payments arm does run an explicit revenue share: integrators tag transactions with an "Integrator ID", and the benefit is a percentage of the TPV of each integrated client. This is Mercado Pago Mexico. I did not confirm an equivalent for Brazilian marketplace integrators. — [Mercado Pago Partnership Program](https://www.mercadopago.com.mx/partners/developers/en/details)

### Inferences
- Plugg.To's partner economics run outward: it pays referral rebates and commissions and offers discounts to referred clients. That matches a SaaS company acquiring sellers through agencies and ERPs, not one that monetizes partners.
- Marketplace partner programs (at least Mercado Livre's) reward integrators with visibility and support, not cash. A marketplace revenue share is unlikely to be a material revenue line for Plugg.To.
- Carrier and freight-intermediary partners could in principle pay referral fees or commissions on freight. No source discloses this for Plugg.To, so treat it as unverified. Freight hubs such as Frenet, Frete Rápido and Melhor Envio are the companies that typically monetize freight. Plugg.To appears to plug into them rather than intermediate freight itself.
- Through Stone/Linx, Plugg.To may contribute to group-level cross-sell (payments, credit, Linx Commerce), and the agency program already sits in Linx's Commerce Partner Program. No source quantifies this.

### Gaps
- Commission and rebate percentages, payment terms and contracts for the Plugg.To and Linx partner programs are not published, and the Partners page returned 503.
- I found no disclosure, positive or negative, of referral fees paid to Plugg.To by carriers, freight intermediaries (Frenet, Intelipost, Frete Rápido) or ERPs.
- I did not check Amazon SPN, Magalu or Shopee partner programs for integrator payouts. I found no evidence either way.

## Q2. What does the Total Express x Plugg.To partnership consist of?

### Takeaway
I could not confirm a 2024-2025 "Total Express x Plugg.To" partnership launch with a plug-and-play app or seller discounts. What exists publicly is a Total Express logistics integration in Plugg.To's help center and Total Express's place in Plugg.To's list of logistics partners. The Total Express seller-partnership news from 2025-2026 is with Melhor Envio (March 2026) and Mandaê, not Plugg.To.

### Cited Findings
- **Help-center article.** Plugg.To's help center has a "Total Express" article under "Integrações Logísticas" titled "Fluxo da integração - Solução de logística Total Express". The page content did not render, so the steps, commercial terms and date are unknown. — [Plugg.To Ajuda – Total Express](https://ajuda.plugg.to/kb/pt/total-express-304702)
- **Partner listing.** Total Express appears in Plugg.To's list of logistics partners. — [Plugg.To – Marketplace de integração](https://plugg.to/marketplace-de-integracao/)
- **Melhor Envio (March 2026).** Total Express became a carrier on Melhor Envio. Sellers can compare prices and delivery times and ship "sem a necessidade de firmar contratos individuais com transportadoras", and can drop parcels at more than 1,500 Total Express points. The article does not mention Plugg.To and gives no revenue-share terms. — [Frota&Cia, 09/03/2026](https://frotacia.com.br/logistica-total-express-sellers/); [Mercado&Consumo, 09/03/2026](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/09/03/2026/logistica/total-express-passa-a-integrar-plataforma-do-melhor-envio-para-atender-pequenos-lojistas/)
- **Mandaê.** Total Express and Mandaê partnered to give small sellers a more competitive freight option. — [Mundo Logística](https://mundologistica.com.br/noticias/mandae-e-total-express-fecham-parceria-para-oferecer-ao-pequeno-lojista-opcao-mais-competitiva-de-frete)
- **Typical setup.** Total Express integrations on other platforms (Vnda, Eccosys, Base.com) work through webservice credentials and a token the seller obtains from Total Express. In other words, the seller holds its own contract with the carrier. — [Vnda](https://ajuda.vnda.com.br/pt-BR/articles/3935862-integracao-com-total-express); [Eccosys](https://eccosys.tomticket.com/kb/integracoes/integracao-com-a-total-express)

### Inferences
- The Plugg.To–Total Express link looks like a standard technical integration where the seller brings its own Total Express contract. It does not look like a negotiated-rate or revenue-share deal. If a 2024-25 press release exists, it was not indexed or findable.
- The premise of a "Total Express x Plugg.To partnership launched around 2024-2025" may be a confusion with the Melhor Envio deal from March 2026.

### Gaps
- I found no press release, launch date or terms (app, discount, revenue share) for a Total Express x Plugg.To deal. The help article body could not be read.

## Q3. How do competitors charge, and is "% of GMV" common among Brazilian marketplace hubs?

### Takeaway
Pure marketplace hubs in Brazil mostly charge a monthly subscription. Tiers are set by SKUs, listings or marketplace accounts (ANYMARKET), or by orders with a per-order overage (Ideris), or as a subscription plus a fixed fee per approved order (Hub2b). Most hide their R$ prices behind sales demos. A percentage of GMV is not the norm for pure hubs. It appears in adjacent models: Olist ERP's "tarifa de venda" of 0.7–0.95% of GMV, the 21–23% commission of Olist's own marketplace-of-marketplaces offer, and Tray's per-sale percentage. Plugg.To itself is described as a fixed monthly fee with no per-integration charge.

### Cited Findings

**Plugg.To**
- **Plans.** Plugg.To sells Basic, Pro and Premium plans with no public prices; buyers have to talk to a consultant. Plugg.To says it "não cobra nenhum valor adicional por integração". — search summary of [Plugg.To hub-integracao](https://plugg.to/hub-integracao/) (article dated 17/10/2022)
- **Hub pricing models.** Plugg.To's own blog describes three models used by hubs:
  - % of sales, e.g. 1% of R$50k a month = R$500
  - per order, e.g. R$1 per sale
  - fixed subscription tiered by volume or revenue

  The blog does not say which one Plugg.To uses. — [Plugg.To blog, 2022](https://plugg.to/hub-integracao/)
- **Third-party comparison.** A third-party comparison describes Plugg.To as a "Mensalidade fixa" not tied to SKU or integration limits, and ANYMARKET as tiers with SKU and marketplace-account limits that require upgrades. The article is undated (copyright 2026). — [Arcos Scale](https://blog.arcosscale.com.br/anymarket-vs-plugg-to-analise-comparativa-custo-escalabilidade/)

**ANYMARKET**
- **Plans.** Standard (6 marketplace accounts, 5,000 SKUs, 5 users), Performance (10 accounts, 10,000 SKUs, 8 users) and Enterprise (20 accounts, 20,000 SKUs, 15 users, "Consultar"). No public prices. — [ANYMARKET planos](https://anymarket.com.br/planos/)
- **Indicative prices.** Secondary sources cite an entry "Start" plan at around R$500 a month (2025 guide) and "a partir de R$399/mês" (2026 article). These figures are not verified on ANYMARKET's own site. — via search summary ([Arcos Scale](https://blog.arcosscale.com.br/anymarket-vs-plugg-to-analise-comparativa-custo-escalabilidade/), [Base blog](https://base.com/pt-BR/blog/melhores-hubs-integracao-marketplaces/))
- **The "15% do GMV" figure.** ANYMARKET's site claims "15% do GMV dos marketplaces do Brasil passa pelo ANY". This is a market-share claim, not a fee. — [anymarket.com.br](https://anymarket.com.br/)

**Hub2b**
- **Plans.** Inicial (1,000 listings), Básico (2,000), Essencial (3,000), Avançado (10,000) and Corporativo (unlimited). Each plan charges "Mensalidade + taxa por pedido": the monthly fee is prepaid, and a per-order fee on approved orders is post-paid.
- **Other terms.** Unlimited marketplaces, orders and users. "Sem custo de Setup e Implementação". No R$ amounts are published.
- Source: [Hub2b planos](https://hub2b.com.br/planos) (© 2025)

**Ideris (IdealeHub)**
- **Plans.** Pricing is based on orders:

  | Plan | Orders/month | Extra order | Channels |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Lite | 500 | R$0.60 | up to 3 |
  | Pro | 1,000 | R$0.55 | up to 6 |
  | Plus I | 3,000 | R$0.40 | unlimited |
  | Personalizado | negotiated | — | — |

- **Other terms.** Annual billing saves 15%. Base monthly fees were not displayed.
- Source: [Ideris planos](https://ideris.com.br/planos)

**Magis5**
- **Plans.** Starter (up to 500 orders a month), Business (more than 500 orders, 2 or more marketplaces, multi-CNPJ) and Enterprise (complex ERPs, custom API). No prices; demo only. — [Magis5 planos](https://www.magis5.com.br/planos) (© 2025)

**Bling (ERP with marketplace integrations)**
- **Plans.** Flat monthly plans tiered by marketplace/API orders:

  | Plan | Price | Marketplace/API orders |
  |---|---|---|
  | Cobalto | R$60/mês | up to 200 |
  | Titânio | R$96/mês | 500 / 2,000 / 5,000 tiers |
  | Diamante | R$357.50/mês | up to 10,000 |
  | Elite | from R$1,250/mês | custom |

  The page also shows conflicting figures in its comparison table (e.g. Titânio "a partir de R$1.440", Diamante "R$7.800"), possibly annual prices.
- **Fees.** The FAQ says there is no extra cost for integrations or invoice issuance. Bling earns transaction fees on payment services (Pix QR R$0.30–0.90, boleto R$1.30–1.95) and sells paid support and onboarding.
- Source: [Bling planos e preços](https://www.bling.com.br/planos-e-precos)

**Olist ERP (formerly Tiny)**
- **Plans.** Avance R$66, Construa R$177, Impulsione R$390, Domine R$948, and Protagonize (custom; a secondary source cites R$5,584). No order-volume limits.
- **Tarifa de venda.** A "tarifa de venda" is charged as a percentage of monthly GMV on confirmed orders, with listed rates of 0.95% and 0.7% (plan mapping unclear) and "sob consulta" for the top tier.
- **Price update.** A secondary source says Olist updated pricing in 2026, with monthly contracts adjusted from 1 June 2026.
- Sources: [Olist planos](https://olist.com/planos/); [Digital Vale, 2026](https://portal.digitalvalecompany.com/qual-e-o-valor-mensal-da-olist/)

**Olist marketplace-of-marketplaces (Olist Store)**
- **Plans.** Crescer at R$39/mês with 23% commission (sellers billing up to R$1M a month), and R$177/mês with 21% commission (R$1M–3M a month).
- **Commission makeup.** The commission is channel fees (around 18%) plus Olist's 3–5%. There is also a R$5 per item freight fee.
- Sources: [Olist solutions plans](https://olist.com/solutions-for-commerce/plans/); [Olist blog](https://blog.olist.com/3-vender-com-olist-quanto-custa-preco-e-comissao/)

**Others**
- **Tray, Oruc and Ideris.** A 2024 hub roundup says Tray charges "um percentual sobre cada venda" on marketplace sales, Oruc advertises "sem cobrança de comissão sobre vendas", and Ideris limits marketplaces and orders per plan and charges for extra orders. — [Oruc blog, 05/03/2024](https://www.oruc.com.br/blog/melhores-hubs-de-integracao-de-marketplace/)

### Inferences
- **Dominant model.** Subscription tiers sized by orders, SKUs or listings, or marketplace accounts, sometimes with per-order overage (Ideris R$0.40–0.60) or a per-order fee (Hub2b). Pure hubs rarely charge % of GMV. Rivals such as Oruc even market "no commission on sales" as a differentiator.
- **Where % of GMV appears.**
  - In ERP and suites: Olist ERP at 0.7–0.95% of GMV.
  - In full-service "seller-of-record" models: Olist Store at 21–23%, which bundles marketplace fees.
  - In e-commerce platforms: Tray.

  A hub charging % of GMV would therefore sit at the "more monetized" end. Plugg.To's own 2022 blog presents 1% of sales as one market option.
- **Pricing transparency.** It is low. ANYMARKET, Hub2b, Magis5 and Plugg.To all gate prices behind sales. Bling and Olist (SMB/ERP) publish prices.
- **Monetization beyond subscription.** In this segment it comes mainly from payments (Bling's Pix and boleto fees, Olist), paid onboarding and support, and ERP bundling, rather than from carrier or marketplace revenue shares.

### Gaps
- No verified R$ prices for ANYMARKET, Hub2b, Magis5 or Plugg.To. The ANYMARKET R$399–500 figures come from secondary sources.
- Ideris base monthly fees were not shown on the page.
- I could not confirm whether Plugg.To currently charges any per-order or GMV-linked component. The planos page returned 503.
- How Olist's 0.95% and 0.7% GMV rates map to its plans is unclear from the page extraction.
